HOW TO PLAY

1
Show one hand

Start the camera and hold up one hand. Single-hand tracking (numHands = 1) locks a virtual knife onto your index finger.

2
Slash through balls

Balls fly up with real projectile physics. Sweep the knife through them — fast swings are detected with swept line-segment collision, so nothing tunnels through.

3
Score, combo, survive

Each cut splits a ball and scores points. Chain cuts fast to grow your combo. Let 3 balls drop and it's game over — restart instantly.
